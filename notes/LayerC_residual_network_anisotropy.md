## The structural question

Does the TAFA package **naturally** produce:

- **A.** residual networks with net scalar anisotropic stress (α_c not ≪1), or  
- **B.** near-isotropic residual networks (α_c→0 after averaging)?

If **A** is generic, low-η requires conspiracy and the expected signal stays O(0.05–0.15).

## Argument from locked structure

### 1. Freeze is global in time, local in space

Pause branch: \(N=0\) when the cosmological field reaches the freeze slope. The **trigger is homogeneous in time** (one activation interval \(z_{\mathrm{tr}}\sim0.3\)), but the **cone network is spatially inhomogeneous** (galaxies, filaments, voids already exist by then).

Residual strain is therefore residual **of a network that already has preferred directions** set by the large-scale structure (LSS) at freeze — not a fresh random lattice painted on a blank sky.

### 2. LSS at z∼0.3 is not orientation-random on all scales

By \(z\sim0.3\), the matter web has:

- coherent filaments and sheets on ∼10–50 Mpc scales,
- tidal fields that align shapes over those scales,
- a preferred direction set by the local tidal eigenframe.

Cone residual elongation, if it couples to the same geometric degrees of freedom that respond to tides (waist / volume-preserving deformation of the network), inherits **tidal alignment**, not pure randomness.

### 3. Tidal alignment ⇒ net scalar projection

Aligned uniaxial residuals in a tidal field produce a **nonzero** longitudinal anisotropic stress in the principal frame of the tide. That is the same mechanism that generates intrinsic alignments of galaxies: the mean is not zero once the tidal field selects a direction.

Order-of-magnitude: intrinsic-alignment physics gives alignment strengths that are small but not parametrically zero — typically projecting O(0.1–1) of the local shape signal onto large-scale tidal tensors. Mapping that analogy here:

\[
\alpha_c^{\mathrm{(tidal)}}\;\sim\;\mathcal{O}(0.1\text{–}1),
\]
not parametrically \(\ll1/3\).

### 4. What would be required for near-isotropy (escape B)

For α_c→0 generically one would need either:

- residual **decoupled** from the LSS tidal field (extra assumption: freeze residual is pure micro-lattice noise uncorrelated with tides), or  
- residual **actively scrambled** after freeze on timescales shorter than the observation, or  
- exact cancellation between regions to a precision tighter than the tidal alignment amplitude.

None of these is forced by the locked volume constraint or pause ontology. They are **additional** isotropy assumptions.

### 5. Verdict on generic vs fine-tuned

| Outcome | Natural under package? |
|---|---|
| Residual couples to pre-existing LSS geometry | **Yes** — network already anisotropic at freeze |
| Tidal alignment → O(0.1–1) scalar projection | **Motivated** by same geometric degrees of freedom |
| Near-isotropic residual (α_c≪1) | **Possible but extra** — requires decoupling from tides |

**Structural claim (filed):** low-α_c is **not** the generic expectation; it requires residual–tide decoupling. The expected regime remains α_c∼O(0.1–1), i.e. |η|∼O(0.05–0.15) after freeze.

This is still not a theorem of a varied action. It is a **naturalness** argument inside the package: the same network that carries the residual is the one already shaped by LSS.
