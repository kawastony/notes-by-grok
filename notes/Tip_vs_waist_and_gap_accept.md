## Does the distinction flip the rate math?

**No.**

- Euclidean barrier rates remain of the form \(\Gamma e^{-S}\).
- Strain bias still enters as work on a displacement: \(S\approx S_0-\beta s\).
- That class yields **logistic** μ, not \(x/(1+x)\).
- Waist strengthens the **two-channel** ontology (active/pause) but does not convert tunneling into linear rates.
- A truly cylindrical (fixed circumference) limit still has an action and Arrhenius rates; it does not force a prefactor \(\propto s\).

The obstruction is **exponential barrier vs linear rate**, not tip vs waist.
