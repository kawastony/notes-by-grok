Tony Kawas / 11 September 2026.

## One-sentence verdict

TAFA’s forced Simple form survives SPARC without being preferred; the first clean quantitative outward discriminator is late-time slip at fixed ΛCDM distances, with amplitude **locked only up to an order-unity constitutive factor** and with the **\(z\sim0.3\) turn-on** as the sharpest claim.

## What is solid

- Residual bookkeeping source term: \((11/72)_{\mathrm{cone}}\approx0.15\) — locked, not tuned.
- Turn-on structure: slip expected after freeze (\(z\lesssim0.3\)), not before — distinct from constant late-time MG templates.
- Competitor contrast: GR and Simple-μ MOND+Λ both predict η≈0; TAFA does not.
- SPARC form/scale tests: draw — derivation bought coherence, not empirical separation.

## What is not locked

- Constitutive map residual strain → observed |η|: still **\(\mathcal{O}(1)\)** factor, unmeasured.
- Therefore observable amplitude is **not** fixed at 0.15; only the source term is.
