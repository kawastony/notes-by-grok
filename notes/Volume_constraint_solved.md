# Volume constraint — solved

Tony Kawas / 7 September 2026. Calculation. Not a finished proof of rolling DE.

Cone: base radius \(R=e^{A(\phi)}\), height \(L\), \(R^2 L=\mathrm{Vol}_0\).

ADM height grows by the lapse: \(\dot L=N\). Warp: \(\dot R/R=A_\phi\,N p\) with \(p=d\phi/d\tau\).

Differentiate the constraint:

$$
N\bigl(2 L A_\phi\, p + 1\bigr)=0.
$$

That is the solved equation. Two branches, no third.

---
