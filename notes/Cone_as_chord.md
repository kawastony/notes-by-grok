# The cone as a chord

Tony Kawas / 6 September 2026. Pattern note. Not a paper. Not a fifth force.

---

## Pattern

The cone is one chord. Wherever it sits, the intervals stay the same.

- Wall (field cannot cross).
- Waist (pause: volume fixed, momentum stored, two clocks).
- Flow on the slope (active phase).
- One field, not a second substance per floor.

A **floor** is only the octave: galactic disk, late sky, a slab, a small laboratory. Changing octave changes the pitch (\(a_T\), \(\Lambda_0\), \(f\)). It does not rewrite the chord.

---

## What this is not

- Not \(Q\). A diary or an intention is not a term in \(G=T\).
- Not one number that must appear at every octave (not 11/72, not 12, not 12.1 kpc as a law).
- Not a proof that the same mass serves DM and DE.
- Not a claim that writing the cone makes the sky obey.

A chord constrains what can happen. It does not play itself.

---

## How this sits on the locked line

- DM floor: same chord, galactic pitch \(a_T\).
- DE floor: same chord; late pitch is B1 (\(\Lambda_0\)), field parked at the midpoint.
- 5D slab: same chord, extra-dimension length \(\Delta y\).

If a later storey exists, it is a new closed string — a new interval — not a louder repeat of this chord.
