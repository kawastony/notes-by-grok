## 1. Circularity — was the interpolator derived or chosen?

**Prediction B** says: at \(g_N=a_T\),
\[
g_{\mathrm{obs}}/a_T=\varphi
\]
for the *simple* interpolator.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Is \(\nu(1)=\varphi\) forced by the simple formula? | **Yes** — pure algebra |
| Is the simple formula forced by cone geometry / 5D action? | **No** — it was selected from the MOND interpolator family |
| Does the cone currently derive simple vs standard \(\mu\) vs others? | **Not shown** |

**Verdict on circularity:**

- SPARC preferring a height nearer \(\varphi\) than 2 is evidence that **simple beats standard \(\mu\)** at the transition under our \(a_T\).
- It is **not** evidence that the cone *predicted* \(\varphi\), because the interpolator was not derived from the cone.
- Scorecard label must be: **conditional support for simple over standard \(\mu\)**, not “cone confirmed.”

Until an action-level argument isolates the simple form, B remains a **consistency check of a choice**, not a derivation test.
