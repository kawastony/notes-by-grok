## Option 1 — Discriminating observables

### 1A. Lensing vs dynamics

Empirical status (literature, not a TAFA fit):
- Galaxy RAR continues from kinematics into weak lensing at the **same** ~10^{-10} m s^{-2} scale (Mistele+2024; KiDS; SPARC+lensing).
- BCG/cluster lensing RARs often need a **larger** characteristic acceleration (~10–20× galaxy scale).

**TAFA with fixed disk \(a_T\):** predicts the **same** dynamical and lensing acceleration from the same baryons at galaxy scales — **same as MOND**, not a discriminator vs MOND.

**vs ΛCDM:** both TAFA-floor and MOND differ from pure halo predictions at low g; that does **not** distinguish TAFA from MOND.

**Verdict:** lensing does not yet separate TAFA disk floor from MOND. Need a TAFA-specific prediction (e.g. redshift-dependent \(a_T\), or a cone-defect scale in lensing residuals).

### 1B. Galaxy clusters

MOND residual missing mass after applying \(a_0\) is still **~ order of baryonic mass** (Bullet Cluster residual ~3.4×10^{14} M_⊙, Famaey 2026).

\[
a_T/a_0 \approx 0.69
\]

→ slightly **stronger** deep boost than standard MOND, so residual is **slightly smaller**, not eliminated.

**Verdict:** locked disk floor alone does **not** outperform MOND on clusters and does **not** match ΛCDM collisionless lensing peaks. Clusters remain a shared problem for pure acceleration-scale models. Headline win **not** achieved.

### 1C. Redshift evolution of \(a_T\)

No derivation yet of \(a_T(z)\) from the cone/5D notes. Without that, high-z JWST/ALMA kinematics cannot falsify a constant floor.

**Open equation (legitimate):** derive \(a_T(z)\) from 5D \(\Delta y\) or freeze/pause dynamics, **then** confront high-z data.

### 1D. Pause feature in rotation curves

Pause branch is cosmological (volume constraint \(N=0\)), not a radial feature in disk RCs. No localized RC “pause” radius predicted.

**Verdict:** pause stays sky-degenerate with \(\Lambda\) until a non-distance observable is named.

## Option 2 — LITTLE THINGS (Oh+2015)

Data: VizieR J/AJ/149/180 table2 (Mgas, Mstar in 10^7 M_⊙, V at Rmax).

Asymptotic BTFR-style test \(M_{\mathrm{pred}}=V^4/(G a)\), \(M_{\mathrm{bar}}=M_{\mathrm{gas}}+M_\star\):

| Scale | median \(\log_{10}(M/M_{\mathrm{pred}})\) | rms |
|---|---|---|
| \(a_T=8.25\times10^{-11}\) | −0.14 | 0.69 |
| \(a_0=1.2\times10^{-10}\) | +0.02 | 0.65 |

N=26 galaxies. Scatter is large (dwarfs, incomplete Mstar for some). Zero-point slightly prefers \(a_0\); both are order-of-magnitude consistent.

**Resolved MAE on full curves:** still needs per-radius Vgas/Vdisk (not in scaled rotdmbar alone). Table2 asymptotic test is the best public completion of the “second sample” without contact authors.

**Verdict:** independent dwarf sample does **not** break the frozen floor; does not prove superiority over \(a_0\).
