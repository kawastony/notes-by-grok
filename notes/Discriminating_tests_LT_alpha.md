## Option 1 — Discriminating observables

### 1A. Lensing vs dynamics

Empirical status (literature, not a TAFA fit):
- Galaxy RAR continues from kinematics into weak lensing at the **same** ~10^{-10} m s^{-2} scale (Mistele+2024; KiDS; SPARC+lensing).
- BCG/cluster lensing RARs often need a **larger** characteristic acceleration (~10–20× galaxy scale).

**TAFA with fixed disk a_T:** predicts the **same** dynamical and lensing acceleration from the same baryons at galaxy scales — **same as MOND**, not a discriminator vs MOND.

**Verdict:** lensing does not yet separate TAFA disk floor from MOND.

### 1B. Galaxy clusters

MOND residual missing mass after applying a_0 is still **~ order of baryonic mass**.

a_T/a_0 ≈ 0.69 → slightly **stronger** deep boost than standard MOND, residual slightly smaller, not eliminated.

**Verdict:** locked disk floor alone does **not** outperform MOND on clusters. Headline win **not** achieved.

### 1C. Redshift evolution of a_T — **tested**

Prototype from pause + a_{(2)}:

\[
a_T(z)=a_T(0)\,H_0/H(z)
\quad\Rightarrow\quad a_T(z{\sim}1)/a_T(0)\approx 0.57.
\]

MUSE-DARK III (2026): a_0|_{z∼1} ≈ 2.61×10^{-10} m s^{-2} ≈ **2.2×** local a_0 (higher, not lower).

**Verdict: disfavored (wrong direction).** See [Highz_test_and_xi_wall.md](Highz_test_and_xi_wall.md).

Sibling a ∝ H matches direction (~1.76 vs ~2.2) but is **not derived** from pause. Constant a_T remains the locked disk law until a forced seasonal rule exists.

### 1D. Pause feature in rotation curves

Pause branch is cosmological, not a radial feature in disk RCs. No localized RC “pause” radius predicted.
