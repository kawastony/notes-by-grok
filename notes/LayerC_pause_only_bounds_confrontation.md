## Redshift-matched published numbers

### \(E_G\) (example literature)

| Sample | \(\bar z\) | \(E_G\) measured | GR prediction (approx) | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GAMA / KiDS | 0.27 | \(0.43\pm 0.13\) | ~0.4 | Overlaps turn-on window |
| LOWZ | 0.31 | \(0.27\pm 0.08\) to \(0.40^{+0.11}_{-0.09}\) | ~0.45 | Window center |
| CMASS | 0.55 | \(0.26\pm 0.07\) to \(0.36^{+0.06}_{-0.05}\) | ~0.40 | **Above** \(z_{\mathrm{tr}}\) |
| DESI-DR1 × KiDS/DES/HSC | 0.15–0.95 | Consistent with Planck GR | Planck \(E_G(z)\) | No strong slip detection |

Typical **fractional** uncertainty on \(E_G\) at low \(z\): **15–30%** (1σ). A pure slip of order 15% would shift \(E_G\) by a comparable fraction only if it couples efficiently — order-unity constitutive factor again. Present data are **consistent with GR**; they do **not** yet exclude an \(\mathcal{O}(0.15)\) effect at high significance in the \(z\lesssim 0.3\) bin alone.

### Parameterized late-time MG (DESI FS+BAO + CMB + DES-Y3 + SN)

| Parameter | Constraint (ΛCDM background) | GR value |
|---|---|---|
| \(\mu_0\) | \(0.05\pm 0.22\) | 0 (deviation form) / 1 (some binning) |
| \(\Sigma_0\) | \(0.008\pm 0.045\) | 0 / 1 |
| \(\eta_0\) | \(0.09^{+0.36}_{-0.60}\) | 0 / 1 |

These are **1σ-class** widths. \(\Sigma_0\) is the tightest (~0.05). An order-unity map from residual strain into \(\Sigma\) or \(\eta\) at the 0.15 level sits near **~2–3σ tension territory if the prefactor is ~1**, and **comfortably allowed if the prefactor is ≲0.3**.

**Important:** standard MG parameterizations often assume evolution **\(\propto\Omega_{\mathrm{DE}}(a)\)** for all late times, not a **step at \(z\sim 0.3\)**. A step-on pause residual is **not** identical to a constant \(\eta_0\) from the CMB to today. Constraints quoted above are therefore **indicative pressure**, not a direct falsification of the TAFA turn-on shape.
