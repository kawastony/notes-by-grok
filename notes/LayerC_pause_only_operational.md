## Operational definition

### Observable class: gravitational slip at fixed expansion history

**Expansion history** \(H(z)\) = flat ΛCDM (pause matches distances).

**Slip:**
\[
\eta(z,k)\;:=\;\frac{\Phi}{\Psi}\;-\;1
\qquad\text{or}\qquad
\gamma_{\mathrm{slip}}\;:=\;\frac{\Phi}{\Psi}.
\]

In GR + ΛCDM with smooth DE: \(\Phi=\Psi\) (no anisotropic stress) → \(\eta=0\).

**TAFA pause-only claim (provisional, to be hardened):**

After freeze, residual wall / cone strain can source a small anisotropic stress in the effective 4D fluid, so
\[
\eta \neq 0
\]
at late times even though \(H(z)\) is ΛCDM-like.

**Simple-μ MOND** does not predict this cosmological slip from a pause mechanism — it has no pause branch.

| Quantity | Status |
|---|---|

Until the strain→Σ map exists, the prediction is **qualitative**:

> Non-zero late-time slip at fixed ΛCDM expansion history.

That is already enough to be **distinct from** Simple-μ MOND and from vanilla ΛCDM, and **testable in principle**.
